🎮 How to Play on Any Device
Computer: WASD/Arrows move, mouse aims, Left Click attacks, 1 to 8 casts equipped skills, SPACE casts your equipped Ultimate, SHIFT dashes.
Mobile/Tablet: Use the left joystick to move. Drag/tap the battlefield to aim. Tap ⚔️ to attack, ⚡ to dash, skill icons to cast, and the purple Ultimate icon to unleash your ultimate.
Skill Books must be purchased, then assigned to an unlocked skill slot from Inventory & Gear. Use the ＋ / − camera buttons to zoom closer or farther during battle. Red navigator lines point toward enemies outside your current screen.
